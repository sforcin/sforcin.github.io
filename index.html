About

background

I'm an aspiring machine learning engineer with a research background spanning robot learning, human-robot interaction, and dimensionality reduction. Most of my work lives where ML theory meets messy real-world constraints: limited compute, physical hardware, noisy sensors, and datasets too large to train on directly.

I'm especially drawn to making machine learning efficient — techniques like Tucker decomposition and dataset distillation that let models learn from a fraction of the data — and to deploying those methods on real systems, from bimanual manipulation robots to embedded sensor networks.

Outside the lab, I'm Vice President of IEEE at UC Riverside and a member of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE).
